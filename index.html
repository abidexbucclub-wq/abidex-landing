The Chairman’s Letter

Dear Author,

Most manuscripts do not fail on the page. They fail in the silence after it — the launch no one planned, the brand no one built, the category no one fought for.

Abidex was founded to end that silence. We are a working society of editors, publicists, and launch strategists who take a small class of authors each season and treat their book the way a great house once did: personally, completely, and without formula.

If your manuscript is finished — or nearly — we would be glad to read your application. The committee reads every one of them, and replies to every one of them.

The Chairman

What Membership Includes

Five disciplines. One complete launch.

Accepted members receive a season plan built around the services their book actually needs — nothing bundled, nothing generic.

01

Publishing & Formatting Guidance

ISBN strategy, interior formatting, cover direction, and distribution setup — done properly the first time, across every major platform.

02

Marketing & Publicity

Media kits, podcast and press outreach, review campaigns, and earned-media positioning for your launch window.

03

Author Brand & Platform

A recognizable author identity: website, newsletter, social presence, and a content rhythm you can actually sustain.

04

Launch Strategy

Pricing, categories, pre-order architecture, and the ninety-day plan that carries a book before and after publication day.

05 — By Invitation

The Residency

Each season, selected members are invited into the Abidex residency — focused working time, direct mentorship, and the full support of the society behind one book. Residency places are limited and are offered only through the Selection Committee.
